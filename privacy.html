Fieldmate — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-06

Fieldmate helps Registered Behavior Technicians (RBTs) log supervised and independent fieldwork hours toward BACB certification. This Privacy Policy describes what information the App processes, where it's stored, and the choices you have.

The Developer does not operate servers to collect your fieldwork data. We do not sell personal information and we do not use advertising.

On-Device Data

Fieldmate is local-first. Your profile (name, BACB ID, address, phone, school, employer), the supervisors you add (name, BACB ID, email, qualification), and every fieldwork hour entry you log are stored in a local database on your device. The Developer does not have access to this data. Your data remains on your device unless you choose to export it or turn on optional cloud sync (see below).

Reports You Choose to Export or Share

Fieldmate can generate PDF and CSV fieldwork reports on your device. These reports include the information you've entered (your name, BACB ID, hours, and supervisor details) so they can be verified by a supervisor. Generating a report happens entirely on your device. Sharing a report (for example, by email or messaging) is something you choose to do using your device's own share sheet — the Developer never receives a copy.

Optional Cross-Device Sync (Google Drive)

Fieldmate offers an optional feature to back up your fieldwork data to Google Drive so it can sync between your own devices (for example, a phone and a Chromebook). This feature is off by default and requires you to sign in with your own Google account.

If you turn it on, Fieldmate only requests access to its own private, app-specific storage area in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope) — it cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive. Your data is uploaded directly from your device to your own Google Drive account; the Developer does not operate the server that stores it and cannot access it. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can disconnect this feature at any time from Fieldmate's Settings, which stops any further sync. Disconnecting does not delete files already saved in your Google Drive app-data folder; you can remove them yourself through your Google Account's connected apps management page.

Data Integrity

While we make every effort to maintain data integrity, we cannot guarantee against loss or corruption. We recommend keeping an independent backup of your data (for example, an exported PDF or CSV report, or the optional Google Drive sync) in addition to the App's local database.

Security

Security of your on-device data depends on your device's own security features (such as a passcode, biometrics, or disk encryption). We recommend enabling these protections.

Data Deletion

You can delete individual entries, supervisors, or your whole profile at any time from within the App, or remove all local data by uninstalling the App. If you enabled Google Drive sync, you can also delete the app-data folder yourself through your Google Account's connected apps page. Because there is no Developer-operated server copy of your data, deleting it from your device (and, if used, from your own Google Drive) deletes it completely.

Analytics and Advertising

Fieldmate does not contain any analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash-reporting code. Nothing you enter, and no record of how you use the App, is transmitted to the Developer.

Children's Privacy

Fieldmate is a professional tool intended for RBTs pursuing BCBA certification and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If Fieldmate's data practices change, this policy will be updated first, and any new feature that changes how your data is handled will require your explicit action to enable.
